<h2>MCNU Land Acknowledgement</h2>
<p>Oakland and
Berkeley are situated in the territory of Huchiun (&ldquo;who-chi-oon&rdquo;),
the ancestral lands of the Lisjan (&ldquo;lee-shawn&rdquo;) Ohlone, the
Indigenous people who have inhabited the East Bay in the San Francisco
Bay Area for thousands of years. The Confederated Villages of Lisjan is
one of many Ohlone tribes in the broader Bay Area. We acknowledge the
land and labor of the Ohlone people, whose connection to this land we
remember, and whose presence- past, present, and future- we respect. As
part of Mills&rsquo; mission of supporting and fostering learning
through the generation and dissemination of knowledge, we acknowledge
that the land we occupy today is the original homeland of Ohlone
people.</p>
